## The mark cap

At most about `maxMarks` marks are drawn (600 by default). Above that, neighbouring items that agree
on every encoded dimension share a mark, `layout.itemsPerMark` says how many, and the description
says so too. Nothing is sampled away: every item is in exactly one mark, and a mark never mixes
categories.

Try 30,000 books below. The layout stays fast, and the print looks like the 600-book one, because
it is drawn with the same number of marks.

## Motion

Marks glide to their new places between views while there are at most `motion.glide` of them (800)
and recolour gently up to `motion.fade` (400). Above those they change at once, because SVG cannot
animate more marks smoothly. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing animates. Pass
`motion={{ glide: 0, fade: 0 }}` to turn it off.

## Ridges

The faint ridges show while a collection is too small for its marks alone to read as a fingerprint
(`show: "auto"`, fading from 60 items to 160), and `ridges={{ show: "always" }}` or `"never"`
overrides that. Their `opacity`, `color` and `width` are yours to set too.
